Specific heat is the quantity of [[Heat]] required to raise the [[Temperature]] of one gram of a substance by $1 \degree C$. The specific heat of a substance is modelled in the equation:
$$c = \frac{Q}{m \Delta T}$$

where $Q$ is heat transfer, $m$ is the mass of the substance, and $\Delta T$ is the change in temperature.
