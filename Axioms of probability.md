1. The probability of an event is a non-negative real number: $P(E) \geq 0 \space \forall E \in F$.
2. The probability that at least one of the elementary events in the entire sample space will occur is $1$: $P( \Omega ) = 1$
3. Any countable sequence of disjoint sets (synonymous with [[Mutual exclusivity|Mutually exclusive]] events) $E_1, E_2, \dots E_n$ satisfies
$$P \left( \bigcup_{i = 1}^{ \infty } E_i \right) = \sum_{i = 1}^{ \infty}{P(E_i)}$$
