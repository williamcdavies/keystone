IEEE 802.11 is part of the IEEE 802 set of LAN technical standards, and specifies the set of MAC and [[Physical layer]] protocols (LYP) for implementing WLAN computer communication.

## 802.11a
- 5 GHz range
- up to 54 Mbps

## 802.11b
- 2.4 GHz unlicensed spectrum
- up to 11 Mbps

## 802.11g
- 2.4 GHz range
- up to 54 Mbps

## 802.11n: Multiple Antennae
- 2.4 / 5Ghz range
- up to 200 Mbps
