Bistable multivibrators are two state devices that maintain a given output state indefinitely until an external trigger is applied. As such, bistable multivibrators are often used to manage single bit memory. The most basic bistable multivibrators are [[Set-reset latches]]. Other bistable multivibrators include:
- [[Data latches]]
- [[Set-reset flip-flops]]
- [[Jack-kilby flip-flops]]
