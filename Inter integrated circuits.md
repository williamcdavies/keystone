$\text{ I }^2 \text{ C } \text{ s }$ are synchronous, multi-controller/multi-target (master/slave), single-ended, serial communication buses. They can be found in a wide range of electronics applications where low manufacturing cost are more important than speed.

The cost of an $\text{ I }^2 \text{ C }$ bus is low due to its capability to control a network of a device chips with two general-purpose I/O pins and software.
