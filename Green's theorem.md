Let $D$ be an open, simply connected region with a boundary curve $C$ that is a piecewise smooth, simple closed curve that is oriented counterclockwise. Let $\vec{F} = \langle P, Q \rangle$ be a vector field with component functions that have continuous [[Partial derivation|Partial derivatives]] on $D$. Then,
$$\oint_C{ \vec{F} \cdot d \vec{r} } = \oint_C{P \space dx + Q \space dy} = \int{ \int_D{(Q_x - P_y) \space dA} }$$
