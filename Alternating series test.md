The alternating series test guarantees that [[Alternating series]] converge if the following conditions are met:
1. $\text{ | } a_n \text{ | }$ decreases monotonically for sufficiently large values of $n$, i.e., $\text{ | } a_{n + 1} \text{ | } \leq \text{ | } a_n \text{ | }$
2. $\lim_{n \to \infty}{a_n} = 0$
