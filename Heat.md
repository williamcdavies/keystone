Heat is the thermal energy transferred between thermodynamic systems due to a [[Temperature]] difference.

For an isolated system, the fist law of [[Thermodynamics]] states that the change in [[Internal energy]] $\Delta U$ of the system is equal to the amount of heat $Q$ supplied to the system minus the amount of thermodynamic work $W$ done by the system and is written
$Q = \Delta U + W$

The heat transfer equation, which is derived from the definition for [[Specific heat]] and the first law of thermodynamics, relates heat transfer to mass, specific heat, and temperature and is written
$$Q = mc \Delta T$$
