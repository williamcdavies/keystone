Consider functions $f(n)$ and $g(n)$ such that $0 \leq c \cdot g(n) \leq f(n)$ for all $n \geq n_0$, where $c$ and $n_0$ are positive constants. This relationship can be expressed in Omega notation as $f(n) = \Omega (g(n))$. $\Omega (g(n))$ can be understood as the set of functions with a growth rate equal to or greater than $g(n)$.
