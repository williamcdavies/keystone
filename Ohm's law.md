Ohm's law states that the [[Electric current]] through a conductor between two points is directly proportional to the [[Electric voltage]] across the two points. Introducing the constant of proportionality, the resistance, one arrives as the three mathematical equations used to describe this relationship:
$$V = IR \qquad I = \frac{V}{R} \qquad R = \frac{V}{I}$$
