An adiabatic process is a type of thermodynamic process in which [[Heat]] transfer $Q$ is $0$

The [[Work]] $W$ done by an ideal gas undergoing an isothermal process can be modelled by $W = -nC_v \Delta T$, where $n$ is the number of moles, $C_v$ is the constant-volume [[Specific heat]], and $\Delta T$ is the change in [[Temperature]].

The change in [[Internal energy]] for an ideal gas undergoing an adiabatic process can be modelled by $\Delta U = -W$, where $W$ is work done.
