A parametric surface is a surface in the Euclidean space $\mathbb{R}^3$ which is defined by a parametric equation with two parameters $\vec{r} : \mathbb{R}^2 \rightarrow \mathbb{R}^3$.

The simplest type of parametric surfaces are given by the graphs of functions of two variables:
$$z = f(x, y), \qquad r(x, y) = ((x, y), f(x, y))$$
