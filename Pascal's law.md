Pascal's law is a principle in [[Fluid mechanics]] that states a change in [[Pressure]] at any point in an enclosed incompressible fluid at rest is transmitted equally and undiminished to all points in all directions throughout the fluid, and the force due to the pressure acts at right angles to the enclosing walls. This principle can be stated mathematically as: $$\Delta p(z) = \rho g \Delta z$$

where:
- $\Delta p(z)$ represents pressure as a function of height $z$.
- $\rho$ represents the [[Density]] of the fluid.
- $g$ represents the acceleration due to gravity.
