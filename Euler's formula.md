Euler's formula states that, for any real number $x$, one has
$$e^{jx} = \cos{x} + j \sin{x}$$

where $e$ is the base of the natural logarithm, $j$ is the imaginary unit, and $\cos$ and $\sin$ are the trigonometric functions cosine and sine respectively. The formula is still valid if $x$ is a [[Complex numbers|Complex number]].

When $x = \pi$, Euler's formula may be rewritten as $e^{j \pi } + 1 = 0$, which is known as [[Euler's identity]].
