An object is said to be in thermodynamic equilibrium when there is no net change in energy within or between systems. Systems in thermodynamic equilibrium are always in [[Thermal equilibrium]], but the converse is not always true. If the connection between the systems allows transfer of energy as [[Internal energy]] but does not allow transfer of matter or transfer of energy as work, the two systems may reach thermal equilibrium without reaching thermodynamic equilibrium.
