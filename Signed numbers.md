Signed numbers are real numbers other than zero. All signed numbers consist of a sign value and a magnitude. The sign value $(+,-)$ determines the positivity of the number. The magnitude determines the absolute value of the number.

## Signed Binary Numbers
There are three forms in which a signed integer can be represented in binary:
1. [[Sign-magnitude]]
2. [[One's complement]]
3. [[Two's complement]]
