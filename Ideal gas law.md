The volume $V$ occupied by $n$ moles of any gas has a [[Pressure]] $P$ at [[Temperature]] $T$. The relationship for these variables
$$PV = nRT = nk_BN_AT = Nk_BT$$

where $R$ is known as the gas constant, $k_B$ the [[Boltzmann constant]], and $N_A$ the [[Avogadro constant]], is called the ideal gas law or equation of state. The ideal gas law allows for a good approximation of the behaviour of many gases under many conditions.

The ideal gas law was first written as a combination of the empirical [[Boyle's law]], [[Charles's law]], [[Avogadro's law]], and [[Gay-Lussac's law]].
