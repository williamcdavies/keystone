An alternating series is a class of [[Series]] whose values are determined by the form:
$$\sum^{ \infty }_{n = 0}{(-1)^n a_n}$$

or
$$\sum^{ \infty }_{n = 0}{(-1)^{n + 1} a_n}$$

with $a_n > 0$ for all $n$. The signs of the general terms alternate between positive and negative. As with all series, an alternating series converges if and only if the associated sequence of partial sums converges.
