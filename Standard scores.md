The standard score is the number of standard deviations by which the value of an observed value is above or below the mean value of what is being observed. Standard scores are commonly called $z$-scores.

If the [[Population mean]] and population standard deviation are known, a raw score $x$ is converted into a standard score by
$$z = \frac{x - \mu }{ \sigma }$$

where $\mu$ is the population mean and $\sigma$ is the population standard deviation.
