To express magnitude, number systems assign positional weight to the numerical position of each digit in a number.

Positional weights for non-fractional digits are modelled with the expression $b^n$ where $n$ represents the numerical position of the digit ascending to the left and $b$ represents the base of the number system being used.

Positional weights for fractional digits are modelled with the expression $b^{-n}$ where $n$ represents the numerical position of the digit ascending to the right and $b$ represents the base of the number system being used.

## Binary Numbers
The right-most bit is called the LSB (least significant bit) and has a weight of $2^0 = 1$. The left-most bit is called the MSB (most significant bit) and has a of $2^n$ where $n$ is the position of the largest non-zero bit or size of storage media.
