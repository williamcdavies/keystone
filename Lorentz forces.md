The Lorentz force is a physical effect that occurs in the vicinity of electrically neutral, electric current-carrying conductors causing moving electrical charges to experience a [[Magnetic fields|Magnetic field]].

The Lorentz force law states states that a particle of charge $q$ moving with a velocity $\vec{v}$ in an [[Electric fields|Electric field]] $\vec{E}$ and a magnetic field $\vec{B}$ experiences a force of
$$\vec{F} = q( \vec{E} + \vec{v} \times \vec{B})$$

It says that the electromagnetic force on a charge $q$ is a combination of a force in the direction of the electric field $\vec{E}$, and a force at right angles to both the magnetic field $\vec{B}$ and the velocity $\vec{v}$ of the charge.
