The permittivity of free space, denoted $\epsilon_0$, is a constant with an exact value of $8.8541878188 \times 10^{-12} F \cdot m^{-1}$. It is a measure of how dense an [[Electric fields|Electric field]] is permitted to form in response to electric charges and relates the unites for electric charge to mechanical quantities such as length and force.
