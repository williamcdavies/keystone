The null hypothesis is the claim that the effect being studied does not exist. The null hypothesis can be described as the hypothesis in which no relationship exists between two sets of data or variables being analysed. If the null hypothesis is true, any experimentally observed effect is due to chance alone. In contrast with the null hypothesis, an [[Alternative hypotheses|Alternative hypothesis]] is developed.
