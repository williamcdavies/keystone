Finite-state automatons are conceptual models used to describe systems with a finite number of states and transitions, and are a notable component of [[Automata theory]]. There are two classes of finite-state automatons, [[Deterministic finite automatons]] and [[Nondeterministic finite automatons]] which can be further divided into acceptors, classifiers, transducers, and sequencers.
