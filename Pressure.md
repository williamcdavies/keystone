Pressure is the force applied perpendicular to the surface of an object per unit area over which that force is distributed. [[Gauge pressure]] is the pressure relative to the ambient pressure. This relationship is modelled by the equation:
$$p = \frac{F}{A}$$

where:
- $p$ represents pressure.
- $F$ represents the magnitude of the normal force.
- $A$ represents the area of the surface on contact.
