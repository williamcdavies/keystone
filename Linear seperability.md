Linear separability is a property of two sets of points. This is most easily visualised in two dimension by thinking of one set of points as being coloured blue and the other set of points as being coloured red. These two sets linearly separable if there exists at least one line the plane with all of the blue points on one side of the line and all the red points on the other side.
