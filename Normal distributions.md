The normal distribution is the continuous probability distribution of a real-valued random variable.

If $X$ is a normally distributed [[Continuous random variables|Continuous random variable]], then the expected value of $X$ is written $E[X] = \mu$. Similarly, the variance of $X$ is written $V[X] = \sigma^2$.

## Probability Mass Function
The simplest case of a normal distribution is the standard normal distribution. This is a special case where $\mu = 0$ and $\sigma^2 = 1$ and is given by the [[Probability mass functions|Probability mass function]]
$$\phi(z) = \frac{e^{ \frac{-z^2}{2} }}{ \sqrt{2 \pi } }$$
