Let $S$ be a [[Linear spaces|Linear space]] or an affine space over the real numbers, or, more generally, over some ordered field. A subset $C$ of $S$ is convex if, for all $x$ and $y$ in $C$, the line segment connecting $x$ and $y$ is included in $C$.

This means that the affine combination  $(1 - t)x + ty$ belongs to $C$ for all $x$, $y$ in $C$ and $t$ in the interval $[0, 1]$.
