A complex number is a number of the form $a + bi$ where
1. $a$ is the real part of the complex number.
2. $b$ is the imaginary part of the complex number

If $b = 0$, then $a + bi$ is real number. If $a = 0$ and $b$ is not equal to $0$, the complex number is called a pure imaginary number. An imaginary number is an even root of a negative number.

## Complex Conjugation
Take a complex number and mirror across the horizontal axis. $\overline{z} = a - bi$

Complex numbers are not ordered, it is not possible to say that one is greater than or less than another.

$z \cdot \overline{z} = \text{ | } z \text{ | }^2$
