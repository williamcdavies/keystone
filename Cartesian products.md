## References
Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications Sec. 2.1.6 - Kenneth H. Rosen
> "Let $A$ and $B$ be sets. The *Cartesian product* of $A$ and $B$, denoted by $A \times B$, is the set of all ordered pairs $(a, b)$, where $a \in A$ and $b \in B$. Hence, $A \times B = \{ (a, b) \mid a \in A \land b \in B \}$."
