For a real linear space, an inner product $\langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle$ satisfies the following four properties. Let $u$, $v$, and $w$ be vectors and $c$ be a scalar, then:
1. $\langle u, v \rangle = \langle v, u \rangle$
2. $\langle cu, v \rangle = c \langle u, v \rangle$
3. $\langle u + v, w \rangle = \langle u, w \rangle + \langle v, w \rangle$
4. $\langle u, u \rangle \geq 0$
5. $\langle u, u \rangle = 0 \leftrightarrow u = 0$
