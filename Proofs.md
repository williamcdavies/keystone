 Proofs are valid [[Arguments]] that establish the truth of a mathematical statement. A proof can use the hypothesis of the theorem, if any, axioms assumed to be true, and previously proven theorems. A proof that supply all steps and the [[Rules of inference]] for each step are called [[Formal proofs]]. However, formal proofs can be difficult to follow. Therefore, proofs designed for human consumption are are almost always [[Informal proofs]].
