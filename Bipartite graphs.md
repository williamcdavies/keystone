## References
Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications Sec. 10.2.4 - Kenneth H. Rosen
> "A simple graph $G$ is called *bipartite* if its vertex set $V$ can be partitioned into two disjoin sets $V_1$ and $V_2$ such that every edge in the graph connects a vertex in $V_1$ and a vertex in $V_2$. When this conditions holds, we call the pair $(V_1, V_2)$ a *bipartition* of the vertex set $V$ of $G$."
