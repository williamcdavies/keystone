A matrix is in row echelon form if it can be obtained as the result of [[Gaussian elimination]]. The term echelon refers to the fact that the nonzero entries of a matrix in row echelon form look like an inverted staircase. A matrix is in reduced row echelon form if it is in row echelon form, with additional property that the first nonzero entry of each row is equal to $1$ and is the only nonzero entry of its column.
