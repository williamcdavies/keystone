## Discrete Random Variables
The expected value of a [[Discrete random variables|Discrete random variable]] is defined as the weighted average of all possible outcomes. This is stated mathematically as
$$E[X] = \sum_{i = 1}^{ \infty }{x_i \cdot p_i}$$

where $x_1, x_2, \dots , x_i$ are the possible outcomes of the random variable and $p_1, p_2, \dots , p_i$ are their corresponding probabilities. 

## Continuous Random Variables
The expected value of a [[Continuous random variables|Continuous random variable]] is defined as the weighted average of all possible outcomes. This is stated mathematically as
$$E[X] = \int_{ -\infty }^{ \infty }{x \cdot f(x) \space dx}$$

where $f(x)$ is the [[Probability mass functions|Probability mass function]] of the distribution of the continuous random variable.
