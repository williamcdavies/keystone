Propositions are declarative statements that are either true or false, but not both.

```
Which of the following statements are true?

1. Washington, D.C., is the capital of the United States of America.
2. Toronto is the capital of Canada.
3. 1 + 1 = 2
4. 2 + 2 = 3
```

> [!example]
Propositions 1 and 3 are true, whereas 2 and 4 are false.

Letters are used to denote [[Propositional variables]]. Propositions that cannot be expressed in terms of simpler propositions are called [[Atomic propositions]]. Propositions that are not atomic propositions are called [[Compound propositions]].
