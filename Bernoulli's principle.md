Bernoulli's principle is a key concept in [[Fluid dynamics]] that relates [[Pressure]], speed and height. Bernoulli's principle states that an increase in the speed of a fluid occurs simultaneously with a decrease in the fluid's potential energy. This relationship is modelled by the equation:
$$P_1 + \frac{1}{2} \rho v_1^2 + \rho gh_1 = P_2 + \frac{1}{2} \rho v_2^2 + \rho gh_2$$
