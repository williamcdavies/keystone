Convection is single or multiphase fluid flow that occurs spontaneously due to the combined effects of material property heterogeneity and body forces on a fluid, most commonly [[Density]] and gravity.

The onset of natural convection is determined by the [[Rayleigh number]] ($\text{ Ra }$). This dimensionless number is given by
$$\text{ Ra } = \frac{ \Delta \rho L^3}{D \mu }$$

where
- $\Delta \rho$ is the difference in density between the two parcels of material that are mixing
- $g$ is the local gravitational acceleration
- $L$ is the characteristic length-scale of convection
- $D$ is the diffusivity of the characteristic that is causing the convection
- $\mu$ is the dynamic viscosity
