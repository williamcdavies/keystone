The Fourier transform is an integral transform that takes a function as input, and outputs another function that describes the extent to which various frequencies are present in the original function.

The Fourier transform of a complex-valued $f(x)$ on the real line, is the complex valued function $\widehat{f( \xi )}$, defined by the integral
$$\widehat{f( \xi )} = \int_{ -\infty }^{ \infty }{f(x)e^{-i2 \pi \xi x \space dx}}, \forall \xi \in \mathbb{R}$$
