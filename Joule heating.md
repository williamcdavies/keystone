Joule heating is the process by which the passage of an [[Electric current]] through a conductor produces [[Heat]].

The most fundamental formula for Joule heating is the generalised [[Electric power]] equation:
$$P = I(V_A - V_B)$$

where $P$ is the power converted from [[Electric energy]] to thermal energy, $I$ is the electric current travelling through the resistor or other element, and $V_A - V_B$ is the [[Electric potential]] across the element.
