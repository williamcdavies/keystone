A parallelepiped is a prism with a parallelogram as base. Hence the volume $V$ of a parallelepiped is the product of the base area $B$ and the height $h$. With $B = \text{ | } \vec{a} \text{ | } \cdot \text{ | } \vec{b} \text{ | } \cdot \sin{ \gamma } = \text{ | } \vec{a} \times \vec{b} \text{ | }$ (where $\gamma$ is the angle between $\vec{a}$ and $\vec{b}$), and $h = \text{ | } \vec{c} \text{ | } \cdot \text{ | } \cos{ \theta } \text{ | }$ (where $\theta$ is the angle between $\vec{c}$ and the normal to the base), one gets $V = \text{ | } ( \vec{a} \times \vec{b} ) \cdot \vec{c} \text{ | }$.
