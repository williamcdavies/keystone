The row space of a matrix $A$ is the [[Linear spans|Linear span]] of its row vectors. 

Let $K$ be a field of scalars. Let $A$ be an $m \times n$ matrix, with row vectors $\vec{r}_1, \vec{r}_2, \dots , \vec{r}_n$. A [[Linear combinations|Linear combination]] of these vectors is any vector of the form
$$c_1 \vec{r}_1 + c_2 \vec{r}_2 + \dots + c_n \vec{r}_n$$

where $c_1, c_2, \dots , c_n$ are scalars. The set of all possible linear combinations of $\vec{r}_1, \dots , \vec{r}_n$ is called the row space of $A$.
