The Berkeley Software Distribution (BSD), also known as Berkeley Unix, is a discontinued Unix [[Operating systems|Operating system]] developed and distributed by the Computer Systems Research Group at the University of California, Berkeley.

As the original BSD became obsolete, the term "BSD" came to refer to its open-source descendants, including [[FreeBSD]], [[OpenBSD]], and [[NetBSD]]. BSD-derived code has been incorporated into Apple's proprietary operating systems, [[macOS]] and [[iOS]].
