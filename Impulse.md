Impulse is the change in [[Linear momentum]] of an object and is given by: 
$$I = \Delta p$$

[[Newton's second law of motion]] states that the rate of change of momentum of an object is equal to the resultant force $F$ acting on the object: 
$$F = \frac{ \Delta p}{ \Delta t}$$ 

so the impulse $I$ delivered by a varying force can be taken as the integral of the force $F$ with respect to time: 
$$I = \int_{t_a}^{t_b}{F \space dt}$$

The SI unit of impulse is the newton second ($N \cdot s$), and the dimensionally equivalent unit of momentum is the kilogram metre per second ($kg \cdot m \cdot s^{-1}$).
