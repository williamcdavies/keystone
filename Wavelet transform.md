A wavelet series is a representation of a square-integrable function by a certain orthonormal series generated by a wavelet.

A function $\psi \in L^2( \mathbb{R} )$ is called an orthonormal wavelet if it can be used to define a Hilbert basis, that is, a complete orthonormal system for the Hilbert space of square-integrable functions on the real line.
