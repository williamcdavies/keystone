The time complexity of an algorithm or [[Data structures|Data structure]] is the amount of computer time required to solve an instance  of the computational problem as a function of characteristics of the input. Time complexity of an algorithm is often expressed asymptotically in big O notation, such as $O(n), O(n \log{n} ), O(n^{ \alpha }), O(n!)$, etc., where $n$ is the size in units of bits needed to represent the input.
