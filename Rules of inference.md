Rules of inference are principles or logical techniques used to derive conclusions from given premises in deductive reasoning. These rules ensure that the conclusions drawn are valid and logically sound.

The rules of inference for propositions are as follows:
1. [[Modus ponens]]
2. [[Modus tollens]]
3. [[Hypothetical syllogism]]
4. [[Disjunctive syllogism]]
5. [[Addition]]
6. [[Simplification]]
7. [[Conjunction]]
8. [[Resolution]]

The rules of inference for quantified statements are as follows:
1. [[Universal instantiation]]
2. [[Universal generalisation]]
3. [[Existential instantiation]]
4. [[Existential generalisation]]
