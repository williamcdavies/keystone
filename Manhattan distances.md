The Manhattan distance, $d_T$, between two points $\vec{P} = (p_1, p_2, \dots, p_n) \text{ and } \vec{q} = (q_1, q_2, \dots, q_n)$ in an $n$-dimensional real coordinate space with a fixed Cartesian coordinate system, is the sum of the lengths of the projections of the line segment between two points onto the coordinate axes. More formally,
$$d_T( \vec{p}, \vec{q} ) = \text{ || } \vec{p} - \vec{q} \text{ || }_T = \sum_{i = 1}^n{ \text{ | } p_i - q_i \text{ | } }$$
