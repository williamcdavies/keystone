An isochoric process is a type of thermodynamic process in which the volume $V$ of a system remains constant.

The work $W$ done by an ideal gas undergoing an isothermal process is $0$.

The change in [[Internal energy]] for an ideal gas undergoing an isobaric process can be modelled by $\Delta U = Q - W = Q$, where  $Q$ is the [[Heat]] transfer, and $W$ is the [[Work]] done.
