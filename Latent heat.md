Latent heat is the energy absorbed or released by a substance during a change in its physical state that occurs without changing its [[Temperature]]. The latent heat of a substance is modelled in the equation:
$$L = \frac{Q}{m}$$

where $Q$ is [[Heat]] transfer and $m$ is the mass of the substance.

The concept of latent heat, includes the [[Latent heat of fusion]] (solid to liquid), the [[Latent heat of vaporisation]] (liquid to gas), and the [[Latent heat of sublimation]] (solid to gas).

The latent heat of fusion for ice, for instance, explains the behaviour of ice to absorb energy without experiencing a change in temperature until it reaches a liquid state.
