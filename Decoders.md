Decoder are a combinational logic circuits that converts binary information from $n$ coded inputs to a maximum of $2^n$ unique outputs; inverse of [[Encoders]].

## $1$-of-$n$ Decoders
A $1$-of-$n$ decoder takes $n$ inputs and has a quantity of outputs equal to $2^n$, i.e., 
- 74xx146 $(3 - 8)$
- 74xx4514 $(4 - 16)$
