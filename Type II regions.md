Type II regions are regions that are bounded by horizontal lines $y = c$ and $y = d$, and curves $x = g(y)$ and $x = h(y)$, where we assume that $g(y) < h(y)$ and $c < d$. Then we can integrate first over $x$ and then over $y$:
$$\int{ \int_R{ f(x, y) \space dA}} = \int_{y = c}^{d}{ \int_{x = g(y)}^{h(y)}{ f(x, y) \space dx} \space dy}$$
