A metropolitan area network (MAN) is a computer network that interconnects users with computer resources in a geographic region of the size of a metropolitan area. The term MAN is applied to the interconnection of [[Local area networks (LANs)]] in a city into a single larger network which may then also offer efficient connection to a [[Wide area networks (WANs)|Wide area network (WAN)]].

The coverage range of a MAN is $1$ - $100$ kilometers.
