Due to atmospheric modelling frameworks often focusing on the smallest particle-size fractions ($<10 \mu \text{m}$) and typically representing particle mass concentrations without detailed information on chemical composition or biological content, the pathways through which wildland fire emissions affect aquatic ecosystems remain poorly understood despite their potential importance. In addition, atmospheric transport models often underestimate the the transport of coarse wildfire particles, because they tend to deposit large particles out o the atmosphere too quickly.
