Encoders are combinational logic circuits that converts a set of $2^n$ decoded inputs to a maximum of $n$ unique outputs; inverse of [[Decoders]].

## $2^n$-to-$n$ Encoders
A $2^n$-to-$n$ encoder takes $2^n$ inputs and has a quantity of outputs equal to $n$, i.e.,
- 74xx148 $(8 - 3)$
- 74xx147 $(10 - 4)$
