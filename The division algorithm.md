Let $a$ be an integer and $d$ a positive integer. Then there are unique integers $q$ and $r$, with $0 \leq r < d$, such that $a = dq + r$. In other words, if the denominator $d$ is greater than $0$ then the numerator $a$ is equivalent to the denominator times the quotient $q$ plus the remainder $r$ where the remainder is greater than or equal to $0$ and less than the denominator.
