The relative permittivity, denoted $\epsilon_r ( \omega )$ or $\kappa$, is defined as
$$\epsilon_r ( \omega ) = \frac{ \epsilon ( \omega )}{ \epsilon_0 }$$

where $\epsilon ( \omega )$ is the complex frequency-dependent permittivity of the material, and $\epsilon_0$ is the [[Permittivity of free space]].

By definition, the relative permittivity $\epsilon_r$ of vacuum is $1$.
