The alternative hypothesis is the claim that the effect being studied does exist. The alternative hypothesis can be described as the hypothesis in which a relationship exists between two sets of data or variables being analysed. If the alternative hypothesis is true, any experimentally observed effect can be explained by the relationship between two variables.. In contrast with the alternative hypothesis, a [[Null hypotheses|Null hypothesis]] is developed.
