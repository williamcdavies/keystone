Entropy is central to the second law of [[Thermodynamics]], which states that the entropy of an isolates system left to spontaneous evolution cannot decrease with time. As a result, isolated systems evolve toward [[Thermodynamic equilibrium]], where the entropy is highest. A consequence of the second law of thermodynamics is that certain processes are irreversible.

The entropy change $dS$ of a system excluding its surroundings can be modelled by $$dS = \frac{ dQ_{ \text{ rev } } }{T}$$
