The handshake lemma states that, in every finite [[Graphs|Graph]], there must be an even number of vertices for which $\deg (v)$ is an odd number as a consequence of the degree sum formula. Vertices of odd degree are called odd nodes. The handshake lemma can be rephrased as the statement that every graph has an even number of odd nodes. The degree sum formula states that the sum of the vertex degrees is equal to twice the number of edges: $\sum_{v \in V} \deg (v) = 2 \text{ | } E \text{ | }$.
