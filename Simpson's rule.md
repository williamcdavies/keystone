Simpson's rule is a method for numerical integration. It is based upon a quadratic interpolation and is the composite Simpson's $1/3$ rule evaluated for $n = 2$. Simpson's $1/3$ rule is as follows:
$$\int_a^b{f(x) \space dx} \approx S_n = \frac{ \Delta x}{3} [f(x_0) + 4f(x_1) + 2f(x_2) + 4f(x_3) + \dots + 4f(x_{n - 1}) + f(x_n)]$$
