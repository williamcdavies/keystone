The set $V$ is said to be a linear space over the real numbers $\mathbb{R}$ if $V$ contains the zero element $0$ and $V$ is closed under [[Linear combinations]], i.e., the linear combination $au + bv$ is contained by $V$ where $u, v$ are arbitrary elements of $V$ and $a, b$ are arbitrary elements of $\mathbb{R}$.
