Analytics is the scientific process of transforming data into insights for making better decisions.

Three developments have spurred the explosive growth in the use of analytics for decision making
- Technological advances - incredible amounts of data from scanner technology, e-commerce, social networks, sensors, and personal electronic devices such as cell phones.
- Methodological developments - faster algorithms can handle and explore massive amounts of data for [[Data visualisation]], machine learning, optimisation, and simulation.
- Explosion in computing power and storage capability - better computational hardware, parallel computing, and cloud computing, enable businesses to solve larger problems faster and with greater accuracy.
