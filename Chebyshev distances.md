Chebyshev distance is a metric defined on a real coordinate space where the distance between two points is the greatest of their differences along any coordinate dimension.

The Chebyshev distance between two vectors or points $a$ and $b$, with standard coordinates $a_i$ and $b_i$, respectively, is
$$D(a, b) = \max_i{( \text{ | } a_i - b_i \text{ | } )}$$
