A function $f$ is said to be bijective if it is both [[Injective functions|Injective]] and [[Surjective functions|Surjective]].

---
## References
Discrete Mathematics and Its Applications Sec. 2.3.2 - Kenneth H. Rosen
> "The function $f$ is a *one-to-one correspondence*, or a *bijection*, if it is both one-to-one and onto. We also say that such a function is *bijective.*"
