## Volume Charge Density
If a charge $Q$ is uniformly distributed throughout a volume $V$, the volume charge density $\rho$ is defined by
$$\rho \equiv \frac{Q}{V}$$

where $\rho$ has units of coulombs per cubic meter ($C \cdot m^{-3}$).

## Surface Charge Density
If a charge $Q$ is uniformly distributed on a surface of area $A$, the surface charge density $\sigma$ is defined by 
$$\sigma \approx \frac{Q}{A}$$

where $\sigma$ has units of coulombs per square meter ($C \cdot m^{-2}$).

## Linear Charge Density
If a charge $Q$ is uniformly distributed along a line of length $l$, the linear charge density $\lambda$ is defined by 
$$\lambda \approx \frac{Q}{l}$$

where $\lambda$ has units of coulombs per meter ($C \cdot m^{-1}$).
