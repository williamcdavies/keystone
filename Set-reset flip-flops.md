Set-reset flip-flops are [[Bistable multivibrators]] similar to gated [[Set-reset latches]]. However, unlike level-sensitive gated set-reset latches, set-reset flip-flops are edge-sensitive. As such, inputs $S$ and $R$ only take effect on a specified edge of a triggering input called a clock ($CLK$).
