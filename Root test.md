The root test is a test for the convergence of [[Series|Infinite series]]. It is dependent upon the quantity:
$$\limsup_{n \to \infty }{ \sqrt[n]{ \text{ | } a_n \text{ | } } }$$

where $a_n$ are the terms of the series, and states that the series converges absolutely if this quantity is less than one, but diverges if it is greater than one. It is useful in connection with [[Power series]].
