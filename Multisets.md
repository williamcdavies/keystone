Multisets (short for multiple-membership [[Sets]]) are unordered collections of elements where elements can occur as members more than once. To avoid the ambiguity between multisets and other forms of sets, an alternate notation is used. The notation $\{ m_1 \cdot a_1, m_2 \cdot a_2, ..., m_r \cdot a_r \}$ denotes the multiset with element $a_1$ occurring $m_1$ times, element $a_2$ occurring $m_2$ times, and so on.
