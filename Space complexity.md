The space complexity of an algorithm or [[Data structures|Data structure]] is the amount of memory space required to solve an instance  of the computational problem as a function of characteristics of the input. Space complexity of an algorithm is often expressed asymptotically in big O notation, such as $O(n)$, $O(n \log{n} )$, $O(n^{ \alpha })$, $O(n!)$, etc., where $n$ is a characteristic of the input influencing space complexity.
