The limit comparison test is a method of testing the convergence of [[Series|Infinite series]].

Suppose that two series $\sum_n^{ \infty }{a_n}$ and $\sum_n^{ \infty }{b_n}$ with $a_n \geq 0, \space b_n > 0$ for all $n$. Then the $\lim_{n \to \infty }{ \frac{a_n}{b_n} } = c$ with $0 < c < \infty$, then either both series converge or diverge.
