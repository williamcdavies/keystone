On the $n$-dimensional [[Euclidean space]] $\mathbb{R}^2$, the intuitive notion of length of the vector $x = (x_1, x_2, \dots , x_n)$ is captured by the formula
$$\text{ || } x \text{ || } := \sqrt{x_1^2 + \dots + x_n^2}$$

This is the Euclidean norm, which give the ordinary distance from the origin to the point $X$ - a consequence of the Pythagorean theorem.
