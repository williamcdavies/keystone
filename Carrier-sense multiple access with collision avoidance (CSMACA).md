Carrier-sense multiple access with collision avoidance (CSMA/CA), is a [[Data link layer]] multiple access method in which carrier sensing is used. Under CSMA/CA, nodes attempt to avoid collisions by beginning transmission only after the channel is sensed to have no traffic. When they do transmit, nodes transmit frames in their entirety.

This technique is primarily used in wireless networks, where the alternative with collision detection [[Carrier-sense multiple access with collision detection (CSMACD)]] is not possible due to wireless transmitters de-sensing their receivers during packet transmissions.

CSMA/CA is unreliable due to the [[Hidden node problem]].
