An isothermal process is a type of thermodynamic process in which the [[Temperature]] $T$ of a system remains constant.

The [[Work]] $W$ done by an ideal gas undergoing an isothermal process can be modelled by $W = Q = nk_BN_AT \ln{[ \frac{V_f}{V_o} ]}$, where $Q$ is the [[Heat]] transfer, $n$ is the number of moles, $k_B$ is the [[Boltzmann constant]], $N_A$ is the [[Avogadro constant]], and $T$ is the absolute temperature.

The change in [[Internal energy]] for an ideal gas undergoing an isothermal process is $0$.
